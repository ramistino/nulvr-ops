## Assessment of proposed v0.2 design

**Source-level design controls present:** two separate layers; exact independent manifest/source pins; closed-bundle semantics; fail-closed handling; no protected verdict or ledger write; no production connectivity; bounded inputs; synthetic-first and independent ALPS release; private staging and stronger filesystem requirements before trusted activation.

**Two remaining acceptance clarifications before an unconditional T2 GO:**

1. **Remove `json_pointer_matches` from this initial lock scope.** A 256-byte regular-expression source and absent dynamic flags do not bound worst-case backtracking time. Launch synthetic v0.2 with typed `json_pointer_equals` (scalar JSON values only), raw `sha256_equals`, and a flat `all` over at most 32 named non-recursive assertions. A regex adapter requires a later separately reviewed, demonstrably bounded engine and budget.
2. **Define pin provenance and complete-bundle identity concretely.** For synthetic CLI trials, owner-preapproved manifest digest and expected commit are independently supplied to the checker and the output remains `OBSERVED`. A self-supplied digest is not independent attestation. Before any trusted verdict writer, require a separately protected immutable approval record/anchor and primary-source evidence retrieval. For closed-bundle enumeration, specify exactly one excluded manifest path when the manifest lives inside the root and reject every undeclared file, symlink and non-regular node. Pin typed assertion schema before implementation.

These are *scope and acceptance clarifications*, not a request for another governance platform. Filesystem TOCTOU hardening remains required before adversarial/trusted filesystem use; the existing mutable-directory PoC is confined to unprivileged synthetic tests.
